## Collection Rules & Fairness Guidelines
- **One Task, One PR:** Keep peer evaluation documentation scoped strictly to `S4-SM-04`.
- **Objective & Evidence-Based:** Base ratings and comments on observed collaboration, PR/task follow-through, communication, and role execution across Sprints 1–4.
- **Accurate Status:** Do not mark evaluations as `Submitted` until actually received from the team member, and do not claim incomplete work as finished.
- **Confidentiality:** Store completed individual evaluations privately/securely per team/instructor guidelines rather than committing sensitive personal ratings publicly if restricted.
